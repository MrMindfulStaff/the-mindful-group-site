The hidden barrier: how lack of reliable transportation prevents workforce participation in Milwaukee's underserved neighborhoods.

In Milwaukee, geography is destiny. The neighborhoods with the highest unemployment rates are often the farthest from job centers. A 15-minute drive becomes an hour-plus bus ride with transfers. For someone starting a training program at 8 AM, that means leaving home at 6 AM — before childcare opens.

The Mindful Group's transportation support removes this barrier directly: bus passes for active participants, rideshare partnerships for emergencies, and driver's education to build long-term mobility. These aren't perks — they're infrastructure. Without transportation support, graduation rates drop and the entire engine slows.
