Exploring the connection between stable housing and sustained employment in workforce development outcomes.

You cannot build a career on an unstable foundation. Research consistently shows that housing instability is one of the strongest predictors of employment dropout. When participants are worried about where they'll sleep next week, they cannot focus on certification exams.

The Mindful Group's housing continuum addresses this directly: emergency shelter partnerships for immediate crises, transitional housing for program participants, and pathways to homeownership for graduates. The goal isn't just to house people — it's to create the stability that makes career development possible.

Milwaukee's affordable housing shortage makes this work harder every year. But the Stellar Engine model allocates 30% of surplus to supportive services, including housing — meaning the system funds its own solution.
